MEET DR. P SRAVANI REDDY

A life of care.
A love for creating.

I'm Dr. P Sravani Reddy, a doctor, a mother, and the maker behind Yarnmori. Alongside my work at the hospital and raising my son, crochet gives me space to slow down, create, and find joy in the smallest details.

As a single mother, I wanted to build something of my own that could grow with us. Yarnmori began in the quiet moments between work and family, with a hook, some yarn, and the hope of turning a personal passion into something meaningful.

Today, I handcraft every Yarnmori piece in Telangana, one stitch at a time. From cheerful flowers to little keepsakes, each creation is made with patience and care, ready to become a thoughtful gift or a small joy in someone's day.
